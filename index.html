How it works

Three visits, start to finish

  1. 1

    Free in-home estimate

    We bring the samples to you, measure every opening, and talk through light, privacy and budget. You get a written price before anything is ordered.

  2. 2

    Built to your measurements

    Your order goes to the manufacturer — or our own workroom for draperies. We keep you posted on the build and delivery window.

  3. 3

    Professional installation

    Our installers hang it, level it, program the motors and clean up. If anything is off, we make it right.

Our quiet specialty

We fix blinds other shops won't touch

Broken cord, dead motor, bent headrail, cracked wand, shade that won't hold? Bring it into the shop on West National Ave. We repair most makes and models — including units we didn't sell and brands that no longer exist.

  • Restring and re-cord lift systems
  • Replace tilters, wands, valance clips and brackets
  • Re-tape and shorten wood and aluminum blinds
  • Motor and remote troubleshooting
  • Ultrasonic blind cleaning
